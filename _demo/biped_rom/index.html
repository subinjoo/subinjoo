Hip Kinematic Structure

Type Diagonal Hip · XY-plane axes
DOF 2 (hip) + 1 (knee) per leg
J1 axis [1/√2, 1/√2, 0]  (Z축 기준 45°)
J2 offset J1로부터 −Y 방향 10 cm
J2 axis [0, 1, 0]  (Y축 = 지면 수직)
Knee [1, 0, 0]  (pitch, sagittal)

Equivalent Motion

J1=θ diagonal swing (roll+pitch combined)
J2=θ yaw (leg rotation about vertical)
Knee=θ flexion/extension (sagittal plane)
